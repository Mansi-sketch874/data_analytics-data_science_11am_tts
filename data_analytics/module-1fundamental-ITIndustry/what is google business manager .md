# What Is Google Business Manager?

Google Business Manager is a centralized way for a company or agency to manage its business information and online presence across Google services. It is commonly used to manage Google Business Profiles, business locations, users, permissions, and performance information.

Google currently uses the name **Google Business Profile** for the business listing that appears in Google Search and Google Maps. The profile can be managed directly through Google Search or Google Maps. Businesses with multiple locations or teams may use a business management organization to manage access and profiles more efficiently.

## What Is a Google Business Profile?

A Google Business Profile is a free listing that allows a business to show important information on Google Search and Google Maps, including:

- business name;
- address and service area;
- phone number and website;
- opening hours;
- products and services;
- photos and videos;
- customer reviews;
- questions and answers;
- updates and special offers.

## Main Uses of Google Business Manager

1. **Manage business information:** Keep contact details, hours, location, and services accurate.
2. **Manage multiple locations:** Organize profiles for branches, offices, or stores.
3. **Control user access:** Add owners or managers and assign appropriate permissions.
4. **Respond to customers:** Reply to reviews and answer customer questions.
5. **Publish updates:** Share announcements, offers, photos, and events.
6. **Monitor performance:** Review searches, calls, messages, website visits, direction requests, and other interactions.
7. **Protect business ownership:** Verify the business and control who can edit its information.

## How to Create and Manage a Business Profile

1. Sign in to a Google Account used for the business.
2. Search for the business on Google or visit the Google Business Profile management page.
3. Select **Add your business to Google** if the business does not already exist.
4. Enter the business name, category, address or service area, phone number, and website.
5. Verify the business using the verification method provided by Google.
6. Add accurate hours, services, description, photos, and logo.
7. Check the profile regularly and update information when it changes.

## User Roles and Permissions

- **Owner:** Has the highest level of control and can manage users and business settings.
- **Manager:** Can edit business information, publish content, respond to reviews, and view performance data.
- **Site manager:** May manage selected location activities with more limited access.

Only give people the level of access required for their work. Remove access when a person leaves the organization.

## Role in Digital Marketing

Google Business Manager helps businesses improve local visibility and communicate with customers. Accurate information, regular updates, good photos, and professional review responses can help customers find and trust a business.

## Role in Data Analytics

Business Profile performance data can help an analyst measure local marketing activity. An analyst can study:

- how customers find the business;
- the number of calls, messages, website visits, and direction requests;
- performance differences between locations;
- changes in customer activity over time;
- review ratings and common customer comments;
- which updates, photos, or offers attract more engagement.

This information can be combined with website analytics, sales records, and advertising data to evaluate marketing performance and make better business decisions.

## Best Practices

- Use the official business name and correct category.
- Keep address, phone number, website, and hours consistent.
- Verify the business and avoid duplicate profiles.
- Upload clear, authentic, and relevant photos.
- Respond politely and promptly to reviews.
- Never publish false information or fake reviews.
- Protect account passwords and grant access carefully.
- Review performance data regularly and record important changes.

## In One Sentence

Google Business Manager is a business-management system used to control Google business information, user access, customer interactions, and performance data from a central place.
