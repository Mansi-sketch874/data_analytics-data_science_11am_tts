# What is Statistics?

Statistics is the branch of mathematics that deals with collecting, organizing, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data. It helps us understand patterns, trends, and relationships in data, and it is widely used in business, science, economics, healthcare, and data analytics.

In data analytics, statistics is used to make sense of raw data and support better decision-making.

---

## Importance of Statistics in Data Analytics

- Summarizes large amounts of data
- Identifies trends and patterns
- Helps in decision-making
- Supports forecasting and prediction
- Measures performance and quality
- Detects anomalies or unusual behavior

---

## Key Terms Used in Statistics for Data Analytics

### 1. Data
Data is a collection of facts, numbers, observations, or information used for analysis.

Examples:
- Age of students
- Sales of a company
- Temperature readings
- Customer feedback

Data can be:
- Qualitative data: non-numeric information like colors, names, categories
- Quantitative data: numerical values like marks, income, height

---

### 2. Population
A population is the entire group of individuals or items that we want to study.

Example:
All students in a college.

---

### 3. Sample
A sample is a smaller part or subset of the population selected for analysis.

Example:
100 students selected from 2000 students in the college.

---

### 4. Variable
A variable is a characteristic or attribute that can change or vary.

Examples:
- Age
- Salary
- Height
- Product rating

Types of variables:
- Quantitative variable: numeric values
- Qualitative variable: categories or labels

---

### 5. Mean
Mean is the average of a set of numbers.

Formula:
Mean = Sum of all values / Number of values

Example:
Data: 10, 20, 30, 40
Mean = (10 + 20 + 30 + 40) / 4 = 100 / 4 = 25

Use in data analytics:
To understand the average performance or trend.

---

### 6. Median
Median is the middle value when the data is arranged in ascending or descending order.

Example:
Data: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50
Median = 30

If there are even numbers of values, median is the average of the two middle values.

Use in data analytics:
Useful when data contains outliers.

---

### 7. Mode
Mode is the value that appears most frequently in a dataset.

Example:
Data: 2, 4, 4, 5, 6, 4, 7
Mode = 4

Use in data analytics:
To find the most common value or category.

---

### 8. Range
Range is the difference between the highest and lowest value in a dataset.

Formula:
Range = Maximum value - Minimum value

Example:
Data: 12, 18, 20, 25, 30
Range = 30 - 12 = 18

Use in data analytics:
To measure spread of the data.

---

### 9. Standard Deviation
Standard deviation measures how much values differ from the mean.

- Low standard deviation: data points are close to the mean
- High standard deviation: data points are spread out

Use in data analytics:
To understand variability and consistency in data.

---

### 10. Variance
Variance is the average of the squared differences from the mean.

Use in data analytics:
To measure how spread out the data is.

---

### 11. Percentile
A percentile tells us the value below which a certain percentage of observations fall.

Example:
The 90th percentile means 90% of the data lies below that value.

Use in data analytics:
Used in rankings, performance measurement, and distribution analysis.

---

### 12. Correlation
Correlation measures the relationship between two variables.

- Positive correlation: both increase together
- Negative correlation: one increases while the other decreases
- Zero correlation: no clear relationship

Example:
More study hours may correlate with higher marks.

Use in data analytics:
To identify relationships between variables.

---

### 13. Regression
Regression is a statistical method used to predict one variable based on another.

Example:
Predicting sales based on advertising spend.

Use in data analytics:
Used for forecasting and predictive analysis.

---

### 14. Probability
Probability is the chance or likelihood of an event happening.

Example:
Probability of getting heads in a coin toss = 1/2.

Use in data analytics:
Helps in risk analysis, prediction, and decision-making.

---

### 15. Distribution
Distribution shows how data values are spread out.

Common distributions:
- Normal distribution
- Binomial distribution
- Poisson distribution

Use in data analytics:
To understand the shape and behavior of data.

---

### 16. Frequency
Frequency is the number of times a value or category appears in the data.

Example:
In a class of 30 students, 8 students scored 80 marks.
Frequency of score 80 = 8

Use in data analytics:
Helps build tables, charts, and summaries.

---

### 17. Outlier
An outlier is a value that is much higher or lower than most other values in a dataset.

Example:
In salaries: 25,000, 28,000, 30,000, 32,000, 80,000
80,000 is an outlier.

Use in data analytics:
Outliers may show anomalies or errors in data.

---

### 18. Hypothesis Testing
Hypothesis testing is a method used to test whether an assumption or claim about data is true.

Example:
Testing whether a new marketing strategy increases sales.

Use in data analytics:
Used to make evidence-based decisions.

---

### 19. Sampling
Sampling is the process of selecting a subset from a population for analysis.

Types of sampling:
- Random sampling
- Stratified sampling
- Systematic sampling
- Cluster sampling

Use in data analytics:
Helps reduce cost and time while studying large populations.

---

### 20. Descriptive Statistics
Descriptive statistics summarize and describe the main features of a dataset.

Examples:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Range
- Standard deviation

Use in data analytics:
To explain what the data looks like.

---

### 21. Inferential Statistics
Inferential statistics uses sample data to make conclusions about a larger population.

Examples:
- Confidence intervals
- Hypothesis testing
- Prediction

Use in data analytics:
To make general decisions from limited data.

---

### 22. Central Tendency
Central tendency refers to the middle or typical value in a dataset.

It includes:
- Mean
- Median
- Mode

Use in data analytics:
To find the general trend of data.

---

### 23. Variability
Variability shows how spread out or inconsistent the data is.

Measured by:
- Range
- Variance
- Standard deviation

Use in data analytics:
To understand consistency and risk.

---

### 24. Data Visualization
Data visualization is the representation of data using charts, graphs, and dashboards.

Examples:
- Bar chart
- Pie chart
- Line graph
- Histogram
- Scatter plot

Use in data analytics:
To present findings clearly and quickly.

## Conclusion

Statistics is one of the most important foundations of data analytics. It helps analysts collect, measure, summarize, and interpret data to draw meaningful conclusions. Understanding key statistical terms like mean, median, mode, variance, correlation, probability, and sampling is essential for effective data analysis and decision-making.
